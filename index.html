Abstract

I am a data analyst with an economist's training. Over four years in consulting, at a junior enterprise, Alvarez & Marsal and Peers Consulting + Technology, I have built machine learning pipelines, pricing models and decision tools for large Brazilian companies. This paper presents five of those projects, plus six public repositories, in a common format: setting, approach, result. The headline finding is that the expensive part of most business problems is not the model but the messy input, and that structuring it well pays off quickly: in the strongest case, reviewing 1,000 invoices went from about a week of consultant time to ten minutes.

Keywords: Python, SQL, Databricks, deep learning, document extraction, pricing, LLM agents, business consulting.

JEL codes: C45 (neural networks), C55 (large data sets), D40 (pricing), L81 (retail), M21 (business economics).

3Projects

3.1Invoice extraction with deep learning

Alvarez & Marsal, Disputes & Investigations, 2024–2025

Setting
A financial dispute required reviewing tens of thousands of Brazilian invoices (notas fiscais), photographed in different layouts across every state. Each consultant reviewed about 1,000 invoices per week by hand.
Approach
I trained a deep learning model that reads the invoice photos, extracts and classifies the relevant fields, and writes them to a structured spreadsheet ready for analysis.
Result
1,000 invoices processed in about ten minutes instead of a week, freeing the team to spend its time on the analysis rather than data entry (Figure 1).
Stack
Python, deep learning, Excel output.

On the same team I also worked on NLP, web scraping and dispute analytics.2

3.2Pricing engine for on-demand home services

Peers Consulting + Technology, 2026

Setting
Independent repair technicians (appliances, electrical, plumbing, HVAC) rarely publish prices, so a home-services business had no market reference for what to charge.1
Approach
I built a Python MVP, starting with refrigerator repair, that compares several pricing strategies. I also designed the pipeline that feeds it market evidence: define the search, gather internet price signals, extract and standardise them, validate data quality, then pass them to the recommendation step.
Result
A working prototype. Along the way I found that the visit fee was leaking into every strategy and making them return identical prices, traced the cause and fixed it.
Stack
Python, pandas, web search and places APIs.

3.3Competitor intelligence agent

Peers Consulting + Technology, 2026, in development

Setting
Consulting teams rebuild competitor maps from scratch for each client. The goal is a reusable tool where only the configuration and a sector catalogue change between clients.
Approach
A nine-step LLM pipeline: configuration, discovery, list validation, public APIs, web collection, automatic validation, normalisation, human review and output. Attributes come from a two-layer catalogue, 28 generic plus 10 for food retail. Every fact carries its source, so the comparative view can be audited.
Result
Full design and prompts complete; pilot scoped on Brazilian cash-and-carry retail using only public sources (BrasilAPI, CVM open data).
Stack
Claude API, Databricks, Python, Excel output.

3.4Diagnostic of a product launch process

Peers Consulting + Technology, 2026

Setting
Launching a new product at a large insurer took too long, and nobody agreed on where it stalled.1
Approach
I turned a large round of stakeholder interviews into a structured evidence base, cross-checked every entry against the raw notes, and mapped pain points to actions.
Result
78 validated evidence records, an impact-effort matrix with a 16-item implementation roadmap, and a 37-step target launch flow for executive review.
Stack
Excel, PowerPoint, process mapping.

3.5Case-study generator

Peers Consulting + Technology, 2026

Setting
Marketing needed project write-ups for the website and institutional deck, but drafting each one from internal project files took hours and the formats kept breaking.
Approach
A Python generator that turns a project one-pager into site and deck versions. It checks character limits, measures whether text fits each slide using font metrics, refuses to invent figures, and places client logos from a 135-logo library, choosing the background automatically.
Result
Tested end to end on four real cases across Strategy, Cybersecurity, Tech Strategy and Analytics.
Stack
Python, python-pptx, PDF rendering, Claude skills.
